# Problem brief template

The artefact for the phase-3 exit where the problem will not hold. Much shorter than a shape doc — its job is to make the next probe cheap, not to propose work. Write to `.context/problem-brief-<slug>.md`.

```markdown
# <Title> — problem brief

**Outcome of shaping:** the problem could not be stated well enough to shape against.
Shaping now would have carried a mis-stated anchor to the betting table.

## Best current problem statement

<The sharpest form reached in session, with what remains fuzzy called out.>

## What we eliminated

| Option critiqued | What its rejection taught about the problem |
|---|---|
| <option> | <lesson> |

## Cheapest confirming signal

<The least expensive observable that would confirm the pain is real — an existing
data-lake query, a conversation with N users, a tiny probe. Not a build.>

## Next step

<Who does what to obtain the signal, and what result would justify re-running /shape.>
```
